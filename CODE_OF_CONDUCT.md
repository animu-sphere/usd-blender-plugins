# Code of Conduct

## Scope and expectations

This policy applies to this project's issues, pull requests, discussions and
other official community spaces, and when representing the project publicly.
Everyone participating is expected to:

- Treat people respectfully, regardless of background, identity or experience.
- Discuss technical decisions with evidence and constructive feedback.
- Accept corrections and respect other participants' boundaries.
- Protect private information and share others' work only with permission.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption and publication of private information without consent
are not acceptable. Disagreement about an implementation is welcome; attacks
on a person are not.

## Reporting

Contact a repository maintainer privately through a contact method listed on
their GitHub profile. If no private contact is listed, open an issue only to
request a private contact channel. Do not include allegations, private
information or evidence in that public request.

Include the location of the incident, what happened and any relevant evidence
you may share. If the report concerns a maintainer, contact another maintainer
where possible. Report software vulnerabilities using
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review reports, protect reporter privacy to the extent possible,
and avoid sharing identifying details unless necessary to address the report.
A maintainer involved in an incident should not decide its outcome.

Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include a request to
correct behavior, removal of content, a warning, or a temporary or permanent
restriction from project spaces. Serious incidents may warrant an immediate
restriction. Participants may request reconsideration privately, providing
relevant additional information.
